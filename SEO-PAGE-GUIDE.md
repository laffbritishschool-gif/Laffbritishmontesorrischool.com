# Laff British Montessori School — SEO and Page Usage Guide

Official website: https://laffbritishmontessorischool.com/

This guide describes the six public pages included in the website sitemap. Keep page descriptions, navigation and search snippets consistent with each page's actual purpose.

## 1. Home — https://laffbritishmontessorischool.com/
**Purpose:** Introduce the school and help each visitor choose their next step.

**How visitors use it:** Parents and guardians learn what the school stands for, see the learning journey and school-life photographs, then follow links to About, Gallery, News, Contact or Student Results.

**Search focus:** Laff British Montessori School, Montessori school, early years education, primary school, learning and character development.

## 2. About — https://laffbritishmontessorischool.com/about/
**Purpose:** Explain the school's educational approach, values, care, programmes and relationship with families.

**How visitors use it:** Families considering the school read about its learning philosophy, early-years and primary stages, values and wellbeing approach. They can then explore the Gallery or contact the school.

**Search focus:** About Laff British Montessori School, Montessori-inspired learning, early years, primary education, school values.

## 3. Gallery — https://laffbritishmontessorischool.com/gallery/
**Purpose:** Show authentic visual examples of school life and learning.

**How visitors use it:** Parents and other visitors browse classroom activities, early-years experiences, participation and community moments to better understand the school's environment. Each image should have a concise, accurate alt description.

**Search focus:** Laff British Montessori School gallery, classroom learning, school activities, early years learning.

## 4. News & Events — https://laffbritishmontessorischool.com/news/
**Purpose:** Share public school updates, stories, celebrations and community highlights.

**How visitors use it:** Families and visitors read current updates, follow links to related Gallery or About content, and return to the homepage for other school information. Publish dated, original updates here when available.

**Search focus:** Laff British Montessori School news, school events, school updates, learning activities.

## 5. Contact — https://laffbritishmontessorischool.com/contact/
**Purpose:** Give families a clear route to make enquiries and request admissions or visit information.

**How visitors use it:** Parents and guardians use the school's verified contact channels and visit details to ask questions. Ensure the page displays the school's real telephone, email and address; do not publish placeholders.

**Search focus:** Contact Laff British Montessori School, school enquiries, admissions information, school visit.

## 6. Student Results — https://laffbritishmontessorischool.com/result/
**Purpose:** Provide an access-controlled route for a student or parent/guardian to view a result published by the school.

**How visitors use it:** The user enters the result serial number and PIN supplied by the school, selects the academic session and term, and submits the form. This is a private utility, not a public marketing page. Do not put student names, results, PINs or other personal data in page titles, descriptions, URLs, structured data or analytics.

**Search focus:** Laff British Montessori School result portal, check student result.

## Search-engine setup

- Each public page should have its own descriptive HTML title, concise meta description, canonical URL and social sharing metadata.
- robots.txt allows public crawling and points crawlers to /sitemap.xml.
- sitemap.xml lists the six canonical public pages. Update lastmod values when page content materially changes.
- assets/seo.js keeps page metadata and Schema.org structured data aligned with each page. It identifies the school and page relationships without inventing an address, phone number, accreditation or other unverified facts.
- Keep internal links descriptive and ensure the main navigation and footer connect the public pages.
- Use meaningful image alt text; compress images and provide descriptive filenames where practical.
- Keep visible page headings aligned with the page title and actual content. Avoid keyword stuffing or duplicate boilerplate.
- Do not add a fake site-search action: the result checker is not a general website search engine.
- Submit https://laffbritishmontessorischool.com/sitemap.xml in Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools, then monitor indexing and search performance.

## Important launch checks

1. Confirm that all six canonical URLs load successfully and resolve to the intended page.
2. Confirm the contact page contains real, verified school contact information.
3. Confirm the result page is not exposing private student information to search crawlers.
4. Validate structured data and sitemap in the relevant webmaster tools.
5. Metadata improves clarity and eligibility for search features; it cannot guarantee ranking or immediate indexing.
