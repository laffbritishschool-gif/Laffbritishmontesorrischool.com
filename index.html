About our school

A place where children learn, belong and grow.

Laff British Montessori School brings learning, care and character development together in a purposeful school experience. We support children from early years through primary level with opportunities to discover ideas, practise skills, communicate confidently and become more independent.

✦

Active learning

Children engage with practical activities, conversation, movement and purposeful classroom work.

Our learning journey

Learning for every stage.

Our programmes are structured to help learners build strong foundations, develop confidence and take increasing responsibility for their learning.

01

Early Years

Play Group – KG

Early literacy, numeracy, movement, communication, practical life and discovery through meaningful experiences.

02

Primary 1 – 3

Core foundations

Building academic confidence while developing creativity, communication, problem-solving and positive learning habits.

03

Primary 4 – 6

Growing independence

Strengthening understanding, study habits, critical thinking and readiness for future academic challenges.

04

Beyond the classroom

Sports, arts, ICT, creativity and wider experiences that help children discover interests and strengths.
