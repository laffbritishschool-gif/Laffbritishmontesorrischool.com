A little about us

A place where children learn, belong and grow.

Laff British Montessori School supports children as they build strong foundations, discover new ideas and develop the confidence to participate. Our approach values academic learning alongside practical skills, wellbeing and good character.

01
Meaningful everyday learning

Opportunities to ask questions, practise skills and understand the world through experiences.

02
Care that recognises each child

Encouragement and supportive routines that help children feel ready to learn.

03
Character alongside academics

Respect, responsibility, curiosity and independence are part of the learning journey.

Our learning journey

Support for every stage.

As children grow, their learning evolves. We encourage progress through age-appropriate experiences, strong foundations and opportunities to build independence.

01

Early Years

Play Group – KG

Early literacy, numeracy, language, movement and practical activities that invite children to explore.

02

Primary 1 – 3

Building foundations

Strengthening core skills, communication, problem-solving and positive habits for learning.

03

Primary 4 – 6

Growing independence

Developing understanding, responsibility, study routines and readiness for future challenges.

04

Beyond the classroom

Creativity & discovery

Space for creativity, movement, collaboration and the discovery of personal interests.
